About StraightWay Global Institute

StraightWay Global Institute is a professional educational platform dedicated to promoting psychological well-being, emotional intelligence, and personal growth through interactive workshops and awareness programs.

Founded by Ms. Fathima Mishfah, MSc in Health Psychology (Cardiff Metropolitan University, UK), the institute focuses on making psychology accessible and practical for everyday life. Our workshops integrate the latest psychological research with creative, evidence-based approaches such as art therapy, counselling psychology, and mindfulness.

StraightWay Global Institute is officially CPD Approved.
Our programmes meet internationally recognised Continuing Professional Development (CPD) standards, ensuring quality, credibility, and professional relevance.

Our Vision

To be a trusted and globally connected platform that empowers individuals through psychological knowledge, promoting mental well-being, compassion, and lifelong growth.

Our Mission

  • Conduct impactful, research-informed psychological workshops
  • Provide a safe, inclusive learning environment
  • Promote mental health literacy through accessible education
  • Collaborate globally for cross-cultural knowledge exchange

Our Team
