About StraightWay Global Institute

About Us

StraightWay Global Institute is a professional education and career development institute dedicated to promoting psychological well-being, emotional intelligence, and personal growth through interactive workshops and awareness programs. The institute focuses on guiding students and young professionals toward clear, structured career pathways through specialized courses, skill programs, and personalized consultations.

Built on the values of clarity, growth, and global opportunity, StraightWay Global Institute empowers learners to make informed decisions and progress confidently toward their future. With an emphasis on modern, globally relevant education and trusted mentorship, the institute serves as a bridge between ambition and achievement.

Founded by Ms. Fathima Mishfah, the institute focuses on making psychology accessible and practical for everyday life. Our workshops integrate the latest psychological research with creative, evidence-based approaches such as art therapy, counselling psychology, and mindfulness.

At StraightWay Global Institute, we believe that true learning happens through connection — between knowledge and experience, mind and heart, people and purpose.

Vision

To be a trusted and globally connected platform that empowers individuals through psychological knowledge, promoting mental well-being, compassion, and lifelong growth.

Mission

  • To conduct impactful, research-informed psychological workshops that inspire emotional awareness and resilience.
  • To provide a safe, inclusive learning environment that nurtures personal and professional development.
  • To promote mental health literacy through accessible education and creative therapeutic approaches.
  • To collaborate globally for cross-cultural knowledge exchange.
  • To lay the foundation for a multidisciplinary institute specializing in psychology and human development.

Core Values

  • Empathy – Centering compassion and understanding in every program.
  • Integrity – Committing to ethical, evidence-based practices and confidentiality.
  • Growth – Encouraging continuous learning and personal transformation.
  • Innovation – Combining science with creativity through modern psychological tools.
  • Connection – Building bridges between global knowledge and local communities.

Objectives

  • Conduct professional and community-based psychology workshops, art therapy sessions, and training programs.
  • Develop and certify short-term courses in applied psychology, counselling, and emotional wellness.
  • Collaborate with international universities and experts for academic and research-oriented partnerships.
  • Offer capacity-building and mental health literacy programs for schools, organizations, and institutions.
  • Expand to become a multidisciplinary global education network focused on human growth and innovation.

Mode of Delivery

At this initial stage, StraightWay Global Institute will deliver interactive online psychological workshops and certificate programmes to individuals, educators, and organisations.

All sessions will be conducted through a secure online platform using live and recorded modules. Participants will receive certificates of completion upon successful participation in workshops.

As the institute grows, hybrid and in-person training options will be developed in alignment with Sri Lankan regulatory standards and international educational partnerships.

Future Outlook

While the current focus remains on psychological workshops and short-term programs, the institute envisions evolving into a recognized international center for psychological education, research, and professional development.

Guided by its vision and slogan — 'Connecting Knowledge Across the World' — StraightWay Global Institute aims to bridge academic excellence and human understanding, empowering minds to inspire meaningful change globally.
